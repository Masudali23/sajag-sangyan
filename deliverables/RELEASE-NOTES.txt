WHAT'S IN SAJAG 2.0

Checking (Track E)
- Paste, type, share or speak a financial message. The first check runs on the device.
- Quoted warning signs, a purpose label (promotion, education, both or unclear), "Evidence & gaps" and links to relevant SEBI, RBI and government guidance. Results are never a "safe" verdict.
- Optional AI review, with permission for each message: masked text, retrieval over 22 curated guidance cards and Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite, with exact-quote checks and a second confirmation pass. The AI can clear eligible on-device false alarms, which stay visible as context. Strong results cannot be withdrawn.

Learning (Track C)
- 15 lessons in English, Hindi and Bengali: 7 money concepts (risk, diversification, volatility, compounding, fees, NAV, nomination) and 8 scam lessons, each with an everyday analogy and a short quiz.
- 4 interactive fictional scam stories: a "digital arrest" call, a part-time "like" job, a VIP stock-tips group and a cashback QR code.
- Money simulator with fictional tokens: a sudden dip, an uneven journey and the effect of borrowing.

Access
- English, Hindi and Bengali screens (Bengali in beta). Hinglish and Banglish input.
- Listen reads results, lessons and stories aloud; Speak it turns speech into editable text. Both use the device's speech services.
- After the first sign-in, the on-device check, lessons, stories and simulators work offline.
- Accounts: email and password, with a one-time email code to verify a new account. Each account has its own notebook; saving and cloud backup are always the user's choice.
- Android: Back closes any open menu first, then returns to Home.


RESULTS ON 150 HELD-OUT TEST MESSAGES
(75 risky and 75 harmless, across English, Hindi, Bengali, Hinglish and Banglish)

- With AI review: 149 of 150 correct (99.3%); 74 of 75 risky flagged; 0 of 75 harmless flagged.
- Offline, on the device: 124 of 150 correct (82.7%); 57 of 75 risky flagged; 8 of 75 harmless flagged.
- The AI review completed for 145 of 150 messages; the other five used the on-device result and are included above.

The test messages are synthetic, and the results are not a real-world guarantee.


PRIVACY

- No stock tips, predictions, product promotion or commissions. No SMS reading, and no bank, card or UPI codes are ever requested.
- AI review only with permission for that message; only the masked message is sent, and masking is best effort.
- AI submissions follow Google's data terms (the free tier may use them to improve its products). The notebook stored on the device is not encrypted.

Sajag gives education and warning signs, not investment advice.
